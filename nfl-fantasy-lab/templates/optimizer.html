When you've uploaded a real FanDuel export, every QB/RB/WR/TE candidate also has to clear this week's matchup rules before the optimizer will consider them: QB must be facing a bottom-10 pass defense (most pass yards allowed/game); RB must be facing a defense that's bottom 10 in both rush yards allowed/game and rush touchdowns allowed/game; WR must see at least 25% of their team's own targets/game and be facing a defense that's bottom 15 in both pass yards allowed/game and pass touchdowns allowed/game; TE must have scored at least one receiving touchdown this season (a proxy for a TD-friendly role) and face that same bottom-15 pass defense; DEF is only eligible when this week's opponent offense is bottom 10 in both interceptions thrown/game and sacks allowed/game — a turnover-prone offense with a poor line sets up easy defensive scoring. Anyone carrying any injury designation — including Questionable — is excluded outright. If too few WR, TE, or DEF clear their rule to fill the roster, that position alone falls back to the full real slate rather than leaving the lineup unbuildable. A lineup also never pairs a team defense with a QB/WR/TE from the team it's playing against that week, since a defensive score comes directly at that offense's expense.

The Top-3 Vegas games strategy instead builds the whole lineup from the three games on the slate with the highest over/under (47+ totals tend to produce big QB scores). QB: the best runner among starters facing a bottom-10 pass defense. WR: the best 25%+ target-share WR on the QB's team, the best WR on the team he's playing, and a team's WR2 from one of the other two games. RB: at least one lead back from a team implied to score 27+, and at least one back at $6,500 or less whose team's lead back is out. TE: only those targeted inside the 10. DEF: an opponent implied to score under about 20, then preferring one facing a backup QB, a line that allows a lot of sacks, and playing at home. The picks behind each rule are listed above the lineup.

The Combined strategy uses every game on the slate. The Top-3 Vegas games rules pick the QB, his WR stack, the opposing WR, both RB rules and the DEF; every other slot comes from players in any game who clear the matchup rules above.

The Alpha Rule uses only the three games with the highest over/under among those kicking off Sunday at 1pm and 4pm ET. QB: the starter on the team implied to score more in the game with the highest over/under. WR: his WR1 and the WR1 of the team he's playing; a WR1 facing a top-10 pass defense gives way to his team's WR2. RB1: the fill-in for an injured lead back. RB2 (and RB1 when no lead back is out): a lead back on a team that's favored or implied for 24+, preferring one facing a team whose QB1 and/or WR1 is out, then the weaker rush defense. WR3: from the second or third game, never a team starting a backup QB; the WR1 or WR2, whichever role the defense has given up more to, with an 18%+ target share. TE: on a team implied for 23+, with targets inside the 10, ranked by target share and how many points the defense allows TEs. DEF: the one facing the offense implied to score the fewest points, never one facing a player the rules picked. No more than four players come from one game; FLEX is the optimizer's choice.
