When you've uploaded a real FanDuel export, every QB/RB/WR/TE candidate also has to clear this week's matchup rules before the optimizer will consider them: QB must rank top 15 at the position in per-game passing yardage and passing touchdowns, and be facing a bottom-10 pass defense; RB must rank top 15 in per-game rushing yardage and rushing touchdowns, and be facing a defense that's bottom 10 in both rush yards allowed/game and rush touchdowns allowed/game; WR must rank top 15 in per-game receiving yards, targets, and touchdowns, and be facing a defense that's bottom 15 in both pass yards allowed/game and pass touchdowns allowed/game; TE must rank top 15 in per-game receiving yards and touchdowns, and face that same bottom-15 pass defense. Anyone carrying any injury designation — including Questionable — is excluded outright. These are strict, intersecting filters, so especially early in a season it's normal for them to leave too few eligible players at a position to build a lineup at all.
